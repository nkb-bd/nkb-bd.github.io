**Fluent Forms** is the WordPress form builder I build and lead engineering on at
WPManageNinja. It runs on **700K+ active sites**, and the free plugin is public on the
[WordPress.org plugin directory](https://wordpress.org/plugins/fluentform/).

Unlike the standalone tools I own end to end, this is product work at scale — I've
architected and maintained large parts of it: the form engine, payments,
conversational forms, quizzes, inventory, and save-and-resume, plus validation across
PHP / JavaScript / REST and a long list of third-party SaaS integrations.

## Why it's here

Most of my day-to-day engineering lives in this codebase, and much of what I write
about — backward compatibility, migration safety, and the cost of a small shortcut
across hundreds of thousands of sites — comes straight from maintaining it.

For the case studies and the reasoning behind specific pieces of the work, see
[selected work](/work).
